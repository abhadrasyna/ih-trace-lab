## Definition and method

Conviva ([Concurrent Plays](https://docs.conviva.ai/sei/video/metric-dictionary/concurrent-plays/)): per 60-second bucket, the active sessions with at least one video frame; the metric is the maximum
bucket value in the interval. Pause, rebuffering and seek time is not playing time, and unsuccessful attempts are excluded.

Here: playing time runs from a `PLAYING` state to the next `BUFFERING`, `PAUSED`, `STOPPED` or `DESTROY` state, or a `PLAYER_SEEK`, `PLAYER_EOF` or `PLAYER_ERROR` event. A session counts in every UTC
minute that overlaps its playing time. The chart plots the maximum per 15-minute interval; the summary is the maximum of the 1,440 minute buckets.

## Known gaps and assumptions

- `PLAYING` is a proxy for "video frame rendered"; real frame data is not available.
- Conviva's inactivity limit for stale sessions is not documented in the page, so sessions with no closing event end at their last event (headline) or last event + 300s (sensitivity).
- The local ZA data is VOD-only (`abr-vod-` session ids); linear sessions are not covered.
- The state vocabulary (`PLAYING`, `BUFFERING`, `PAUSED`, `STOPPED`, `DESTROY`, `PLAYER_SEEK`, `PLAYER_EOF`) is ZA's; other opcos must be verified.
- No Conviva reference number for this day is available to compare against.
